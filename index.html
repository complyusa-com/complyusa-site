Why now

Your NIST SP 800-171 obligations are in force today

DoD paused the CMMC Phase 2 certification requirement in July 2026 pending a reform review. DFARS 252.204-7012, NIST SP 800-171 and your SPRS self-assessment still apply. The low-risk move is to confirm where you stand now.

Your SPRS score is an attestation

A NIST SP 800-171 self-assessment score posted to SPRS is a representation to the government. An independent mock assessment confirms it holds up before anyone else checks.

Primes are vetting their supply chain

Prime contractors are asking subcontractors for evidence of NIST SP 800-171 compliance. An independent assessment record answers that question directly.

Be ready when certification resumes

DoD's reform review may change the timeline, not the standard. A mock today shows where you stand against all 320 objectives, so certification is a short step when your contracts require it.

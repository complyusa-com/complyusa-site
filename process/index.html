Process

A defined, five-step assessment

A predictable sequence designed for defense contractors and their assessment teams. No mystery, no scope creep.

Typical timeline: 4–10 weeks from kickoff to final report, depending on scope and readiness.

  1. 01

    Scoping call

    A short call to confirm the assessment boundary, in-scope assets, CUI flows, and stakeholders.

  2. 02

    Readiness / mock review

    Independent review of your System Security Plan, policies, and supporting documentation against NIST SP 800-171.

  3. 03

    Assessment

    Examine evidence, interview personnel, and test controls against all 110 NIST SP 800-171 requirements (320 objectives).

  4. 04

    Findings & report

    Each objective documented as MET, NOT MET or not applicable — with traceable evidence and SPRS-aligned scoring.

  5. 05

    Certification path

    Final assessment record and supporting artifacts ready for the official CMMC Level 2 certification engagement.
